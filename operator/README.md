## 🎯 Core Operating Principle

> **Fixed Camera Golden Rule**: CourtVision uses perspective homography anchored to physical court lines. Once court calibration is complete, the camera must remain completely stationary. If the camera tripod is bumped or moved, re-run `⚙ CALIBRATE COURT`.
