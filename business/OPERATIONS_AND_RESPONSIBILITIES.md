# CourtVision 🎾 — Operations & Responsibility Matrix

Clear division of responsibilities ensures seamless coordination between CourtVision operators, venue owners, and sponsor partners.

---

## 👥 Responsibility Assignment Matrix (RACI)

| Operational Activity | CourtVision Operator | Venue Owner / Manager | Sponsor Partner |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Provide Venue Access & Power** | Informed | **Accountable (A)** | - |
| **Provide Camera Mounting Location** | Consulted | **Accountable (A)** | - |
| **Submit Transparent PNG Logo** | - | - | **Accountable (A)** |
| **Hardware Setup & Calibration** | **Responsible (R)** | Informed | - |
| **Live Session Processing & Ad Switch** | **Responsible (R)** | - | - |
| **Match Video Recording & Backup** | **Responsible (R)** | Informed | - |
| **Sponsor Report Generation & Delivery** | **Responsible (R)** | Informed | Informed |

---

## 🎯 Service Level Agreement (SLA) Targets

- **System Availability / Uptime**: `99.5%` during live match play.
- **Max Ad Switching Latency**: `< 0.5 Seconds` (Zero match delay).
- **Player Protection Accuracy**: `100%` sharp player pixels preserved.
- **Report Delivery Window**: Within `24 Hours` post-event.
