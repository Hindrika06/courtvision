# CourtVision 🎾 — Sponsor Proof of Execution & Post-Match Reporting

Sponsors receive a comprehensive Proof of Execution Package after every match session to verify broadcast airtime and visual rendering quality.

---

## 📦 Proof of Execution Package Components

1. **Post-Match Compliance PDF Report**: Summary of airtime, match duration, sponsor package, and frame counts.
2. **High-Resolution Frame Screenshots**: 5 representative screen captures showing sponsor ad rendered on court with players walking across.
3. **Full Match MP4 Video Recording**: Complete playable recorded video stored in `output/session_YYYY-MM-DD_HHMMSS.mp4`.
