# CourtVision 🎾 — Sponsorship Packages & Pricing Tiers

CourtVision enables venue owners and tournament organizers to offer premium digital court surface advertising tiers.

---

## 🔄 Ad Rotation & Display Rules

1. **Fixed Placement Rule**: Active sponsor graphic remains anchored in perspective space on the court surface throughout set play.
2. **Mid-Match Rotation**: Rotating sponsor packages switch seamlessly during game changes or set breaks using **📢 ADS MANAGER** without interrupting video recording or lag.
3. **Multi-Sponsor Support**: Up to 4 distinct sponsor decals can be rendered simultaneously on different court quadrants (`Court 1`, `Court 2` profiles).
