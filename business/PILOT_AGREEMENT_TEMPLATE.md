# CourtVision 🎾 — Venue Commercial Pilot Agreement Template

This Commercial Pilot Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **CourtVision Operator** ("Operator") and **Venue Partner** ("Venue Owner").

---

## 📄 Pilot Terms & Conditions

### 1. Scope of Pilot Deployment
Operator agrees to install and operate the CourtVision Virtual Advertising System at Venue Owner's facility (`Court 1` / `Court 2`) for a pilot duration of **30 Days** (or 2 Weekend Tournament Events).

### 2. Commercial Revenue Share
All gross digital sponsorship revenues collected during the pilot period shall be split:
- **60% to Venue Owner**
- **40% to Operator**

### 3. Equipment & Property Rights
All CourtVision hardware, camera mounts, and software remain the exclusive property of Operator. Venue Owner provides facility access, tripod mounting space, and 120V AC electrical power.

### 4. Player Protection & Quality SLA
Operator warrants that virtual advertisements will be rendered exclusively on court floor surfaces using pixel-level segmentation to guarantee player visibility and racket protection.

### 5. Termination & Sign-off
Either party may terminate the pilot upon 7 days written notice. Upon completion, Venue Owner may elect to convert to a permanent multi-year commercial license.
