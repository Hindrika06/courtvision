# CourtVision 🎾 — Return-on-Investment (ROI) & Revenue Model

CourtVision transforms single-use tennis and badminton courts into scalable digital advertising assets.

---

## 💰 Tournament Revenue & Profit Split Model

### Per Weekend Tournament Revenue Breakdown (1 Court, 4 Sponsors)
- **1 Headline Baseline Sponsor**: `$1,500`
- **2 Dual Corner Sponsors** (`$1,000` each): `$2,000`
- **1 Secondary Rotating Sponsor**: `$750`
- **TOTAL TOURNAMENT REVENUE**: **$4,250 USD**

### Revenue Distribution Model
- **Venue Owner Share (60%)**: `$2,550` per event
- **CourtVision Operator Share (40%)**: `$1,700` per event

---

## 📈 Payback Period & ROI Calculation

```text
Initial Hardware CAPEX:            $1,125 USD
```
