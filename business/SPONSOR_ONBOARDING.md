# CourtVision 🎾 — Sponsor Asset Onboarding Guide

This document defines the asset onboarding guidelines for sponsors submitting digital logo graphics to be rendered on virtual tennis and badminton court surfaces.

---

## 🎨 Asset Specifications

```text
Format:             PNG (Transparent background required) or High-Res JPG
Resolution:         Minimum 1920 × 1080 pixels (or 2000 × 1000 for rectangular logos)
Color Mode:         RGB / sRGB
Aspect Ratio:       2:1 (Standard Rectangular Floor Decal) or 1:1 (Square Logo)
File Naming:        sponsorname_adtype_YYYY.png (e.g. redbull_baseline_2026.png)
Maximum File Size:  15 MB per graphic
```

---

## 📋 Sponsor Asset Intake Workflow

```text
Sponsor Submits Artwork (PNG)
           ↓
Technical Review (Verify resolution & transparency)
           ↓
Court Surface Simulation Test (Verify perspective warp & floor contrast)
           ↓
Opacity Optimization (Set default 0.85 floor sticker texture)
           ↓
Copy to CourtVision `ads/` Asset Folder
           ↓
Sponsor Sign-off / Approval
```

---

## ⚠️ Floor Graphic Rules

1. **High Contrast**: White, yellow, bright red, or dark black logos provide optimal visibility against green, blue, or clay court surfaces.
2. **Transparent Margins**: Avoid solid rectangular white background boxes around logos unless explicitly requested; transparent backgrounds allow court line textures to show through naturally.
3. **No Dynamic Flash**: Graphics remain fixed to the court surface; non-distracting sponsor visuals respect player focus during live play.
